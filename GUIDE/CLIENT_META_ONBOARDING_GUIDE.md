## 🌟 Welcome to BrandFlow!

BrandFlow lets you design festive greetings, promotional offers, and marketing graphics, then publish them **directly to your Facebook Page and Instagram feed with a single click**.

To make this magic work, Meta (the company that owns Facebook & Instagram) requires a **one-time setup**. 

> [!NOTE]  
> Meta's official security rules do **not** allow automated posting to personal Facebook profiles (like your personal friend timeline). Automated posting is only allowed on **Facebook Business Pages** and **Instagram Professional Accounts**. Both are **100% free to create and take only 60 seconds**.

# 🚀 PHASE 1: Account Setup (Only if you don't have them yet)

---

### 🅰️ Step A: How to Create a Facebook Business Page (60 Seconds)

*(Perform this on your mobile phone in the Facebook App)*

1. Open the **Facebook App** on your phone.
2. Tap the **Menu icon** (the **three horizontal lines `☰`** or your profile picture with three lines):
   - On **Android**: Located at the **top-right** corner.
   - On **iPhone (iOS)**: Located at the **bottom-right** corner.
3. Tap on the **"Pages"** option (look for the orange flag icon 🚩).
4. Tap the **"+ Create"** button at the top-left of your screen.
5. Tap the blue **"Get Started"** button.
6. **Enter Page Name:** Type the official name of your business or clinic (e.g. *Apex Dental Clinic* or *Sharma Sweets*) and tap **"Next"**.
7. **Select Category:** In the search box, type your business type (e.g. *Doctor*, *Restaurant*, *Shopping & Retail*, *Hair Salon*) and tap on the best match. Tap **"Create"**.
8. Facebook will ask if you want to add phone, website, or address:
   - You can enter your details now, or simply tap **"Next"** ➔ **"Next"** ➔ **"Done"**.

🎉 **Congratulations! Your Facebook Business Page is live!**

---

### 🅱️ Step B: How to Switch Instagram to a Free Professional Account (45 Seconds)

*(Perform this in the Instagram App on your phone)*

1. Open the **Instagram App** on your phone.
2. Tap your **Profile picture** in the **bottom-right** corner to open your profile page.
3. Tap the **Menu icon** (the **three lines `☰`**) in the **top-right** corner.
4. Tap **"Settings and privacy"** (or **"Settings"**).
5. Scroll down to the section titled **"For professionals"** and tap **"Account type and tools"**.
6. Tap **"Switch to professional account"**.
7. Tap the blue **"Continue"** button through the 4 intro slides.
8. Choose a **Category** that describes your work (e.g., *Restaurant*, *Doctor*, *Clothing Store*) and tap **"Done"**.
9. When asked *"Are you a Creator or Business?"*, select **"Business"** and tap **"Next"**.
10. Review your contact info and tap **"Next"**.
11. On the *"Set up your professional account"* screen, simply tap the **"X"** (close) icon in the top corner to return to your profile.

🎉 **Your Instagram is now an official Professional Business Account (100% Free forever).**

---

### 🆎 Step C: How to Connect Instagram to Your Facebook Page (30 Seconds)

*(This links both accounts together so BrandFlow can publish to both simultaneously)*

#### ⭐ Method 1 (Recommended — Direct via Facebook "Linked Accounts"):
*This is the most reliable official method recommended by Meta.*

**On Mobile (Facebook App):**
1. Open the **Facebook App**.
2. Switch to your Business Page: Tap **Menu (`☰`)** ➔ tap your **Page Name** (or tap the switch icon next to your name).
3. Once on your Page profile, tap the **Settings icon (⚙️ gear)** in the top-right corner.
4. Tap on **"Linked Accounts"**.
5. Tap on **"Instagram"**.
6. Tap the blue **"Connect account"** button ➔ tap **"Continue"** / **"Confirm"**.
7. An official Instagram login screen will appear: Enter your Instagram username and password ➔ tap **"Log in"**.
8. Tap **"Confirm"**. You will now see your Instagram username with a green confirmation mark!

*(On Desktop/Computer: Go to facebook.com ➔ Switch to your Page ➔ Left sidebar: Settings ➔ Linked Accounts ➔ Instagram ➔ Connect Account).*

---

#### 📱 Method 2 (Alternative — Directly via Instagram App):
*If you prefer doing it from your Instagram app:*

1. Open the **Instagram App** and go to your **Profile** (tap your photo at bottom-right).
2. Tap the **"Edit profile"** button located right below your bio.
3. Scroll down to the **"Public business information"** section.
4. Tap on **"Page"** (or *"Connect or create a Facebook Page"*).
5. Tap **"Connect existing Page"** (log into Facebook if prompted).
6. Select your Facebook Page name ➔ tap **"Done"**.

🎉 **Both accounts are now officially linked together!**

# 🤝 PHASE 2: Connecting to BrandFlow (The 2-Minute Handshake)

Now that your Facebook Page and Instagram are ready, here is how BrandFlow gets connected to publish posts for you:

---

### Step 1: Submit Your Facebook Page in BrandFlow (or via WhatsApp)
You can submit your Facebook Page directly inside BrandFlow:
- Go to **Connections** in BrandFlow (`/connections`).
- In the **"Managed Setup: Submit Your Facebook Page"** box (or in the Setup Guide Modal), paste your Facebook Page link or exact Page name and click **"Submit Page"**.
- *Alternative:* You can also send the link directly to the BrandFlow team via WhatsApp.

> **💡 How to find your Facebook Page link on your phone:**  
> Open Facebook ➔ Go to your Page ➔ Tap the **three dots (`...`)** next to "Manage" ➔ Tap **"Copy link to Page"**.

---

### Step 2: BrandFlow Admin Sends Official Publishing Request
The BrandFlow team enters your Page name and dispatches an official Meta **"Content Management & Publishing"** request.

---

### Step 3: Approve the Request (1-Click)

> [!IMPORTANT]  
> Meta agency partnership requests do **not** appear in standard personal Facebook notifications. You must open the official Meta Requests page directly.

1. Open this official Meta link on your phone or computer browser:  
   👉 **[https://business.facebook.com/settings/requests](https://business.facebook.com/settings/requests)**
2. Log in with your Facebook account if prompted.
3. Click on the **"Received"** tab at the top.
4. You will see a pending request from **Brandflow**.
5. Click **"Approve"**.
6. Review the permissions (**Content & Publishing**) and click **"Approve"** again.
7. Enter your Facebook password when asked to confirm your identity.

🎉 **Setup Finished! You are 100% done!**

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (Client FAQ)

#### Q1: Will BrandFlow have access to my personal messages or private photos?
**No, absolutely not.** BrandFlow only requests **Content Publishing** permissions for your public Business Page. It has zero access to your personal messages, private friends list, personal timeline, or WhatsApp.

#### Q2: Does switching Instagram to a Professional Account cost money?
**No.** Professional and Business accounts on Instagram are **100% free forever**. Instagram does not charge anything for business accounts.

#### Q3: Can I still use my Instagram and Facebook apps normally?
**Yes!** You can use your apps exactly as you always do. BrandFlow simply works as a helper to post your designed graphics automatically without manual uploading.

#### Q4: I opened the Meta link but don't see any request under "Received"?
Make sure of two things:
1. Ensure the BrandFlow Admin has already sent the request.
2. Ensure you are logged into Facebook with the same account that is an Admin of your Facebook Page.
